# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is licensed under CC BY 4.0. The short version is below; the linked text applies where this summary is silent.

## Our pledge
We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Disagree with the data openly. Honest counter-evidence is welcome here.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information (including private repositories and decisions) without permission.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or deliberately fake data.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate. Maintainers will explain the decision.

## Reporting
Report problems privately through GitHub: use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository (it opens a private thread with the maintainers) and begin the title with "Conduct:". Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.
